Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a reservation?

You can make a reservation through our website by selecting your destination, travel dates, and number of guests. After browsing available hotels, click "Book Now" and follow the checkout process.

What is your cancellation policy?

Our cancellation policy varies by hotel. Most properties allow free cancellation up to 24-48 hours before check-in. Please check the specific cancellation policy during the booking process.

Do you offer price matching?

Yes, we offer a best price guarantee. If you find a lower rate for the same hotel and dates on another website, contact us within 24 hours of booking, and we'll match the price.

How do I modify my reservation?

You can modify your reservation by logging into your account and accessing your bookings. Alternatively, you can contact our customer service team for assistance.
